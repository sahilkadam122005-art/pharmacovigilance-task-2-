4. Data Validation Checklist

- Unique case IDs are available.
- Patient information was reviewed.
- Suspect product is recorded in all four cases.
- An adverse event is recorded in all four cases.
- Reported onset and outcomes are documented.
- Seriousness notes were reviewed.
- Missing and uncertain information was identified.
- Follow-up questions were documented.
- Reporter identity and contact information are not provided.
- Dose, route, indication, and exact treatment dates are missing.
- Medical history and concomitant medication details require clarification.

5. Reported Facts and Limitations

The dataset contains four cases involving the reported product Velunex. Hospital admission is noted for PV-102 and PV-104. PV-104 has missing age information and unknown sex.

The available information does not establish that Velunex caused any of the reported events. The time between product use and event onset alone is insufficient to establish causality. Missing information must not be invented.

6. Conclusion

The four sample cases were reviewed for ICSR intake and data completeness. Additional information is needed to improve the quality of the case records, particularly reporter details, product information, medical history, concomitant medicines, and clinical details. Seriousness and causality should be assessed using complete information and applicable pharmacovigilance procedures.
